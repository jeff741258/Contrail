Privacy

  • What's kept. For each flight: the date, airline, flight number, and departure and arrival airports. No names, booking codes, seats, prices or email text.
  • Emails are read in your browser to find flights and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photos are resized and re-saved before they're kept, which removes hidden details such as GPS location.
  • Where it lives. In this browser, and in your own Google Drive, OneDrive or iCloud if you sign in. Contrail has no server and no account of its own; nobody else can see your log.
